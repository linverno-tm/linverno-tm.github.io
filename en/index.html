Hamkor CRM

An installment-sales system that replaced 1C. In development since September 2026; I own the platform.

Four stores ran on four separate 1C databases that couldn’t see each other. A customer blacklisted in one store could get new credit in the next.

I built one system for all of them: sales, installment contracts, payments, cash, warehouse, payroll and reports. It syncs with 1C both ways, so the two ran side by side during the switch.

Works without internet
An encrypted local copy keeps every window usable. Actions queue up and sync back on their own when the line returns.
Windows app
Prints receipts silently and updates itself. Staff work in an app, not a browser tab.
Every release is tested first
Sales, contracts, payments and reports run on a throw-away database before a build goes live.
On the phone too
An Android app for customers is on the way: contract, payment schedule and remaining debt at a glance.

In one month: 1,373 changes, 230+ screens, 65 reports. I can adapt the same platform to your business.
