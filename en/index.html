Hamkor CRM

An installment-sales system that replaced 1C. In development since September 2026; I own the platform.

Four stores ran on four separate 1C databases that couldn’t see each other. A customer blacklisted in one store could get new credit in the next.

I built one system for all of them: sales, installment contracts, payments, cash, warehouse, payroll and reports. It syncs with 1C both ways, so the two ran side by side during the switch.

Works without internet
An encrypted local copy keeps every window usable. Actions queue up and sync back on their own when the line returns.
Windows app
Prints receipts silently and updates itself. Staff work in an app, not a browser tab.
Every release is tested first
Sales, contracts, payments and reports run on a throw-away database before a build goes live.
On the customer’s phone too
Customers see their contract, payment schedule and remaining debt in their own app, shown below under “Mobile apps”.

In one month: 1,373 changes, 230+ screens, 65 reports. I can adapt the same platform to your business.

Hamkor — the customer app

For customers who bought on installments: contracts, remaining debt, next payment and payment history on the phone. They order from the catalog on installments and chat with the store.

One system with Hamkor CRM
A payment made in the store shows up in the app too, nothing to enter twice.
Easy sign-in, solid lock
Sign in with a phone number and an SMS code; after that the app opens with a PIN or a fingerprint.
Three languages, two themes
Uzbek (Cyrillic and Latin) and Russian; light and dark.
Details

Goal

Customers who bought on installments see their contract, next payment and limit without visiting the store, and the store can send them notices and offers directly.

Solution

Flutter and Riverpod, each feature in four layers (model, data, state, screen). Sign-in with an SMS code, the token kept in the phone’s secure storage. Data comes from Hamkor CRM’s customer API. First written in Expo (React Native), fully moved to Flutter on 7 October.

Result

One codebase for Android, iPhone and web. Tests run offline against sample responses. The app is being prepared for the stores.

KINO TV

A bot and mini app for series and films. Sections, search, episode lists; it remembers where you stopped and continues from the next episode.

@linvkino_bot
Details

Goal

Watching long series in Telegram comfortably: find the right episode fast and never lose where you stopped.

Solution

The bot (Python, aiogram 3, SQLite) turns posts in a private storage channel into the catalog on its own: the admin only uploads videos, the bot assigns codes. Videos are sent as copies, with no size limit. The mini app is plain HTML/CSS/JS on Cloudflare Pages, with the catalog cached at the edge.

Result

701 series and films in 8 sections. The mini app continues from the next episode and keeps a saved list.

Qog‘oz Bino

A mini app for a Telegram channel: new episodes, this week’s most watched, search and a personal list.

qogoz-bino.pages.dev
Details

Goal

A poster catalog for a friend’s bot, @QogozBinoTVbot, without touching its code or using its token.

Solution

A script builds the catalog from the bot itself: it sends /start for each code and reads the reply. The mini app searches live in Cyrillic and Latin, filters by section and genre, and opens the bot for the chosen series.

Result

No bot token needed. A full rebuild takes about 10 minutes; new codes are added quickly on their own.

Hamkor Savdo

Site for a four-branch retail chain: catalog, branches and an installment application form.

hamkorsavdo.uz
Details

Goal

Introduce a four-branch chain: the company, the offer, each branch and every way to reach it. The main constraint: fast on slow connections.

Solution

Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind v4 and no animation library: all motion is CSS plus about 1 KB of JavaScript. The logo was traced to vector from the brand book artwork.

Result

The readable page (HTML and CSS) is 35 KB gzipped and works without JavaScript. Every text pair is above 4.5:1 contrast; no invented reviews or facts.
