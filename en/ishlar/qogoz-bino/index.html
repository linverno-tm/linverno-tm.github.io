@QogozBinoTVbot

The problem

The bot had plenty of series, but it ran on the Manybot builder and had no catalog to browse them.

What I built

An own bot in place of Manybot: a menu tree, videos sent as copies from a storage channel, a cover for every series. A script builds the catalog from the bot itself, and a mini app runs on that catalog.

  • Live search in Cyrillic and Latin, filters by section and genre.
  • Picking a series hands over to the bot, which sends the card and episodes.
  • Every episode is at least 480p; vertical and low-quality videos are rejected.

Result

The mini app opens from the bot’s menu button in BotFather; the bot runs on its own server.
